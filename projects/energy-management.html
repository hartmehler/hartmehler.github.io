How does growing electricity demand affect the grid?

For a university presentation, I researched how changes in electricity use relate to generation locations and the transmission infrastructure needed to deliver power.

01 / The questions

Power systems have to work end to end.

Demand

How might electricity use change across homes, business, industry, and transportation?

Generation

How do resource locations and existing supply affect where power can be produced?

Transmission

What infrastructure connects generation to the places that need electricity?

02 / My approach

Synthesize several views of the system.

I organized published projections, maps, and infrastructure figures into a presentation that moves from changing demand to supply geography and transmission constraints. This was research and technical communication, rather than an original power-grid simulation.
